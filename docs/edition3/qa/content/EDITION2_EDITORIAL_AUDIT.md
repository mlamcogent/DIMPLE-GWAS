# Edition 2 editorial audit and Edition 3 reconstruction decision

## Diagnosis

Edition 2 is technically extensive: 24 chapters, 15 appendices, 105,730 source words, 370 rendered pages, 21 figures, 110 inventoried callables, and 35 output patterns. It contains valuable verified material. Its central editorial problem is not missing effort but mode confusion.

Conceptual chapters repeatedly interrupt the reader with complete source-location cards, caller/callee lists, artifact contracts, and inherited “operational foundation / retained evidence / output checkpoint / misinterpretation / boundary” scaffolds. Those structures serve an audit database but make the book feel assembled rather than argued. Similar learning objectives and generic exercises recur across chapters. The same release and interpretation cautions are often restated rather than placed at the scientific decision that needs them.

The Edition 2 QA log itself records 265 normalized exact paragraph groups and 22 high-similarity pairs among 1,296 substantive paragraphs. Its self-assessment regarded much of that repetition as deliberate traceability. Edition 3 accepts the traceability need but relocates it into the reference and evidence layers.

## Material worth preserving

- the mathematical core of the UMAP/densMAP chapter;
- exact preprocessing, search, scoring, ranking, interpretation, comparison, and validation behavior;
- executed fixed-fixture values and their limitations;
- the hand-worked numerical examples;
- function and output inventories;
- historical 2,315/459/164 analysis distinctions;
- validated figures that genuinely teach geometry or workflow;
- the principle that equations must be interpreted immediately in words.

## Material to rewrite, relocate, or remove

| Decision | Edition 2 material | Edition 3 treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve | Verified equations, definitions, fixed-run facts, artifact identities | Rewritten into continuous narrative with updated Stage 1 evidence |
| Rewrite | Chapter openings, transitions, examples, exercises, interpretation | One intellectual journey across 15 chapters |
| Relocate | Function dossiers, source locations, call graphs, CLI field lists, output schemas | Technical reference |
| Relocate | Command sequences, checkpoints, recovery and exercises | Analyst workbook |
| Remove | Repeated scaffolds, stock cautions, duplicated release banners, generic exercises | Replaced by topic-specific reasoning |
| Remove | Edition 2 facts superseded by Stage 1, including the earlier 45-pass phrasing | Stage 1 truth: 44 passed, 1 optional XLSX skip, 5 warnings |

## Calibration decision

The Edition 3 voice is calibrated to a question-driven chapter: begin with a concrete statistical problem, build intuition, introduce formal structure, connect to the exact Freeze 3 implementation, and end by naming the decision the reader can now make. Audit identifiers appear in a compact evidence trail rather than the body of every explanation.

Status: accepted for reader-draft authoring.
