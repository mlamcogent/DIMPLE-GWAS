## Blocking versus non-blocking interpretation

G-001 through G-004 block claims of a complete scientific freeze. G-005 through G-008 limit release provenance and portability. G-009 through G-012 block several reviewer-facing scientific claims. G-013 through G-018 are tractable documentation or artifact-management constraints.

None of these gaps invalidates the bounded local conclusion that the Freeze 3 RC1 software path is executable and internally consistent under the tested environment. Together they require the Stage 1 decision to carry limitations.
