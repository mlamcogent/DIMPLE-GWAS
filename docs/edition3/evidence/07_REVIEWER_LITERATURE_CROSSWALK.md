## Corrected comparator identity

The genomic PCA/ICA paper matching the reviewer discussion is:

> Oblong, L. M., Soheili-Nezhad, S., Trevisan, N., Shi, Y., Beckmann, C. F., & Sprooten, E. (2024). *Principal and independent genomic components of brain structure and function*. **Genes, Brain and Behavior, 23**(2), e12876. <https://doi.org/10.1111/gbb.12876>

The publisher record states that it analyzed two UK Biobank releases of 2,240 IDPs and evaluated genomic PCA/ICA reproducibility. Earlier draft references to an exact Yang/Zhao comparator are not supported by the supplied reviewer materials and must be removed or replaced.

## Response-writing rule

For every reviewer item, the response should label its evidence as one of: completed new analysis, retained/reanalyzed artifact, documented method clarification, planned analysis, or unresolved. Literature belongs in the clarification column unless it is actually used in a new analysis.
